# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Primary users are plant operators, OT security leaders, utility executives, and hackathon judges reviewing a safety-critical workflow on a laptop or projector. Operators need to understand whether a command is held, what evidence exists, which deterministic rule controls the outcome, and whether the plant remains safe. Judges need to grasp the product thesis in under 30 seconds and inspect technical depth without leaving the demonstration.

## Product Purpose

HISN-OT is an Agentic Network-Enforced Safety Gate for Critical Infrastructure. It prevents a privileged digital command from becoming a physical action until network context and deterministic process conditions are valid. The anchor demonstration is a connected MENA desalination facility, with a product path into oil and gas, energy, ports, factories, airports, hospitals, and smart-city infrastructure.

Success is a backend-driven proof: credentials are valid, context is compromised, an unsafe 88% pressure request is blocked against a versioned 60% limit, the operator-facing primary edge is isolated, and the separately enrolled backup becomes owner only after confirmed handover. An unconfirmed handover safe-stops the modeled process.

## Brand Personality

Exacting, composed, consequential. The product speaks with the calm authority of an industrial safety instrument. It avoids fear-based security theatre and makes every assertion traceable to evidence, policy, or measured process state.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a generic SaaS admin template, a cinematic fake terminal, a security operations screen filled with decorative charts, or a marketing landing page disguised as an application. Avoid glassmorphism, gratuitous glow, stock cyber imagery, identical card grids, giant introductory copy, and motion unrelated to persisted system events.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with the physical consequence, then reveal the network proof.
2. Separate requested intent from measured plant reality at all times.
3. Make authority legible: the agent recommends, policy decides, enforcement acts.
4. Show provenance and uncertainty wherever external evidence appears.
5. Preserve presenter control without turning the product into a scripted animation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Every action is keyboard operable with visible focus, every status has text or iconography beyond color, live changes use restrained screen-reader announcements, motion honors reduced-motion preferences, and layouts remain usable at 390 by 844 pixels through 1440 by 900 pixels.
