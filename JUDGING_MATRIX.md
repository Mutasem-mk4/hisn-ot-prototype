## Thirty-second proof

1. Submit the safe 52% change and show the pump/flow response.
2. Submit 88% and pause with the packet held at the gate.
3. Say: “Credentials are valid, but identity is not enough.”
4. Point to 88% requested versus 52% still accepted.
5. Land on `BLOCK_AND_CONTAIN`, then show targeted isolation and the pending Nokia QoD status.
6. Show zero unsafe executions and the modeled safe stop caused by unconfirmed handover.
